About us

Luxury homes, personally found

Holy Homes Realty · Mumbai · Since 2023

Ekta ChawlaFounder · Holy Homes Realty

Ekta Chawla was born in Mumbai and grew up in Dubai, and she brings that international eye for fine living home to the city she loves.

She began her real estate career in 2023 and founded Holy Homes Realty, a boutique consultancy in Mumbai's luxury segment. Since then she has found homes for many well-known faces in the city, quietly and personally.

Every client gets Ekta herself: a shortlist that is handpicked rather than mass-forwarded, honest advice on price and paperwork, and complete discretion from the first visit to the final keys.
